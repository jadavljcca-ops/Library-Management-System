Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the QR attendance and verification system.

How do I check in to the library?

Simply navigate to the Student Portal, login using your enrollment number or registered email, click 'Share Live Location' to verify you are at the library, and then point your camera to scan the library QR code.

Why is sharing my location mandatory?

To maintain campus integrity and prevent student login entries from off-campus. The application verifies your browser's geocoordinates to confirm you are in proximity to the library building.

How does checking out work?

The system uses the same procedure. Scanning the library QR code while you have an active check-in session will automatically log your checkout time and compute your total session duration.

Is the portal accessible on mobile devices?

Yes, the landing page, student dashboard, and admin control panels are designed to be fully responsive. Students can scan QR codes using their mobile browsers without installing any native apps.
